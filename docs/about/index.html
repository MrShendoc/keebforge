About KeebForge

Our Mission

KeebForge was founded with a simple goal: help people craft their perfect typing experience. Whether you're a competitive gamer seeking every millisecond of advantage, a writer looking for that satisfying tactile feedback, or a hobbyist exploring the world of custom builds—we're here to guide you.

The mechanical keyboard market has exploded with options. That's exciting, but it can also be overwhelming. Our team cuts through the noise with honest, hands-on testing and clear recommendations.

What We Cover

  • Best-of Lists: Curated picks for every budget, use case, and preference
  • In-Depth Reviews: Keyboards, switches, keycaps, and accessories tested thoroughly
  • Educational Guides: From beginner basics to advanced modding techniques
  • Deals & Sales: We track the best prices so you don't have to
  • Community Resources: Glossary, sound tests, and comparison tools

Our Commitment to Transparency

We believe in full transparency. When we recommend a product, we tell you exactly why—and we also tell you who it's not for. We're keyboard enthusiasts first, and we'd rather lose a sale than recommend something we don't believe in.

Some of our links are affiliate links, which means we may earn a commission if you purchase through them. This never affects our editorial independence. Read our full Affiliate Disclosure and Review Methodology for details.

The Team

KeebForge is run by a small team of mechanical keyboard enthusiasts who have collectively spent thousands of hours typing, testing, and tinkering. We've built customs, modded budget boards, and everything in between. This isn't just a job for us—it's a genuine passion.
