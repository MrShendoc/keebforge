Beginner's Guide to Mechanical Keyboards

Everything you need to know to get started with mechanical keyboards.

By KeebForge Editorial

Summary

This guide covers the basics of mechanical keyboards: what makes them different, types of switches, keyboard layouts, and how to choose your first mechanical keyboard.

Key Takeaways

  • Mechanical keyboards use individual switches under each key
  • Three main switch types: linear, tactile, and clicky
  • Common layouts: full-size, TKL, 75%, 65%, and 60%
  • Hot-swappable boards let you change switches without soldering
  • Budget options start around $30-50
