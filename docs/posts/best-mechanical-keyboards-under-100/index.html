Best Mechanical Keyboards Under $100

Summary

Our picks for the best mechanical keyboards under $100 include the Keychron K8, Royal Kludge RK84, and Akko 3098B. These boards offer hot-swap, wireless connectivity, and excellent build quality at accessible prices.

🔑 Key Takeaways
  • Hot-swappable boards let you customize switches without soldering
  • Wireless options like Keychron K8 offer great flexibility
  • Gasket-mounted boards under $100 provide premium sound and feel
  • PBT keycaps are worth prioritizing for durability
