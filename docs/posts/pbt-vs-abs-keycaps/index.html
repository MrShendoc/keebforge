PBT vs ABS Keycaps

Understanding keycap materials and when to choose each.

By KeebForge Editorial

Summary

PBT and ABS are the two main keycap materials. PBT is more durable and resistant to shine, while ABS allows for more vibrant colors and smoother textures.

Key Takeaways

  • PBT: More durable, resists shine, textured feel
  • ABS: Smoother, more colors, develops shine over time
  • PBT typically costs more
  • Double-shot legends last longer than printed
  • Thickness matters more than material for sound
