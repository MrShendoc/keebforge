Switch Types Explained

Linear, tactile, and clicky switches—learn the differences and find your perfect switch.

By KeebForge Editorial

Summary

Mechanical keyboard switches come in three main types: linear (smooth), tactile (bumpy), and clicky (bumpy + loud). Each offers a different typing feel.

Key Takeaways

  • Linear: Smooth keystroke, no bump—popular for gaming
  • Tactile: Noticeable bump, no click—great for typing
  • Clicky: Bump with audible click—satisfying but loud
  • Actuation force affects how hard you press
  • Try before you buy if possible
