Keyboard Glossary

Essential keyboard terminology explained

Actuation Force
The amount of pressure required to register a keypress, measured in grams (g) or centinewtons (cN).
Cherry MX
The most popular mechanical switch brand, known for their color-coded switch types (Red, Blue, Brown, etc.).
Gasket Mount
A mounting style where the plate is suspended by gaskets, providing a softer, more cushioned typing feel.
Hot-Swappable
Keyboards that allow switches to be changed without soldering.
Linear Switch
A switch type with smooth, consistent keypress from top to bottom with no tactile bump.
PBT (Polybutylene Terephthalate)
A durable keycap material resistant to shine and heat.
Tactile Switch
A switch type with a noticeable bump during the keypress, providing feedback without the click sound.
TKL (Tenkeyless)
A keyboard layout without the number pad, typically with 87 keys.
