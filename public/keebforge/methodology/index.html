Review Methodology

How we test, evaluate, and recommend mechanical keyboards

Our Testing Process

Every keyboard, switch, and accessory we review goes through hands-on testing by our editorial team. We don't rely on spec sheets or manufacturer claims—we type, game, and work on each product extensively before making recommendations.

Evaluation Criteria

Build Quality (25%)

  • Case materials and construction
  • Flex and rigidity
  • Weight and stability
  • Keycap quality and material
  • Stabilizer performance

Typing Experience (30%)

  • Switch feel and consistency
  • Sound profile (stock and modded)
  • Keycap texture and profile
  • Typing comfort over extended sessions

Features (20%)

  • Connectivity options (wired, Bluetooth, 2.4GHz)
  • Software and customization
  • Hot-swap capability
  • RGB lighting quality
  • Battery life (for wireless)

Value (25%)

  • Price relative to competition
  • Included accessories
  • Upgrade potential
  • Long-term durability

Gaming-Specific Testing

For keyboards marketed toward gamers, we also evaluate:

  • Input latency (wired and wireless)
  • Polling rate consistency
  • N-key rollover and anti-ghosting
  • Performance in competitive scenarios

Best-Of List Criteria

Our "Best Of" lists are updated regularly based on new releases and price changes. Products are selected based on:

  • Hands-on testing by our team
  • User feedback and long-term reliability reports
  • Current market pricing and availability
  • How well they serve the specific use case

Editorial Independence

Our reviews are never influenced by affiliate relationships or advertising. We purchase most products ourselves or receive review units with no strings attached. Manufacturers have no editorial input on our content.

When we receive free review units, we disclose this clearly. Our verdict remains the same regardless of how we obtained the product.

Updates & Corrections

We regularly update our reviews as firmware updates, price changes, or new information becomes available. When we make significant updates, we note the revision date at the top of the article.

If we make an error, we correct it promptly and note the correction.

Questions?

If you have questions about our methodology or want to know more about how we tested a specific product, contact us.
